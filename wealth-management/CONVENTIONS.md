# Output Conventions

## Purpose

This plugin produces **adviser working papers** — structured strategy analysis, calculations, and recommendation summaries that go to **paraplanning** for formal documentation.

The adviser uses this tool to run through a client scenario, model strategies, and produce a **paraplanning brief**. The paraplanning team then uses the brief to draft the formal Statement of Advice (SOA) or Record of Advice (ROA).

## These outputs are NOT

- Client-facing advice documents
- Statements of Advice (SOA) or Records of Advice (ROA)
- Compliant advice documents ready for distribution to clients
- Financial product recommendations requiring PDS references

The adviser and paraplanner remain responsible for ensuring all formal documentation meets AFSL, ASIC, and Best Interest Duty requirements.

## These outputs ARE

- **Adviser working papers** with strategy analysis and rationale
- **Calculations with full workings** — the evidentiary basis for recommendations
- **Paraplanning briefs** — structured inputs for the paraplanning team to draft formal SOA/ROA
- **Internal documents** — not for client distribution without formal documentation

## Paraplanning Brief Structure

Each skill output follows this structure:

### 1. Client Facts & Assumptions
- Key client facts used in the analysis (demographics, income, assets, liabilities, goals)
- All assumptions stated explicitly (growth rates, inflation, tax rates, contribution caps, pension rates)
- Source of client data (fact find date, last review, client-provided)

### 2. Strategy Analysis & Calculations
- What was modelled and why
- Alternatives considered and why they were accepted or rejected
- **Full calculation workings** — not just results, but the steps, formulas, and inputs
- Quantified outcomes (projections, tax savings, comparisons, net benefit)
- Year-by-year projections where relevant

### 3. Recommendations Summary
- Prioritised action items for the client
- Rationale for each recommendation (why this strategy, not just what)
- Risks identified and mitigations proposed
- Implementation steps and timing

### 4. Basis for Advice
- The key calculations that support each recommendation — this is the section paraplanning needs most
- Cross-references to legislative provisions where relevant (e.g., s961B Best Interest Duty, TR 2000/2 interest deductibility, SIS Act contribution caps)
- Sensitivity analysis and stress testing results
- Comparison of recommended vs current position (quantified difference)

### 5. Paraplanning Notes
- Items for the paraplanner to address in the formal SOA/ROA:
  - Product research required (APL compliance, PDS references, platform fees)
  - Disclosure requirements (fee schedule, conflicts, remuneration)
  - Risk profile confirmation (does the strategy match the client's documented risk profile?)
  - Areas where the adviser needs to make a judgement call or provide further input
  - Any information gaps that need to be resolved before the SOA can be finalised

## Calculation Standards

- **Show workings**: every calculation must show inputs, formula, and result — not just the answer
- **State assumptions**: growth rates, inflation, tax rates, contribution caps, pension drawdown rates — all stated explicitly at the top of each analysis
- **Current legislative thresholds**: use current rates and caps (update annually or when legislation changes)
- **Financial year alignment**: all tax modelling uses the Australian financial year (1 July - 30 June)
- **Nominal and real values**: show both where relevant (especially retirement projections over 10+ years)
- **Marginal vs average tax rates**: use marginal rates for strategy benefit calculations, average rates for overall tax burden
- **Rounding**: calculations to the nearest dollar, percentages to two decimal places
